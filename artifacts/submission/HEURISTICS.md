# Insider Trading Heuristics

The investigation does not attempt to prove insider trading.

Instead, it prioritizes wallets for manual investigation using multiple independent signals.

## H1 — Pre-public-event purchases

Purchases occurring before verified public disclosure events receive higher weight.

## H2 — Repeated profitable outcomes

Wallets consistently purchasing winning outcomes across multiple unrelated markets receive higher scores.

## H3 — Conviction

Large position sizes relative to the wallet's activity indicate higher conviction.

## H4 — Low-probability winner entries

Buying outcomes that were priced as low probability but later resolved as winners increases suspicion.

## H5 — Public event timing

Purchases shortly before verified public events receive additional weight.

## H6 — Cross-market consistency

Repeated success across independent market families increases investigative priority.

## H7 — Control market comparison

Performance in externally observable control markets is compared against disclosure-sensitive markets to distinguish general trading skill from potential informational advantage.

No individual heuristic is considered evidence of insider trading. The final ranking combines all available evidence.
